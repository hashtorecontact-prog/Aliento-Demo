Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Aliento (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, hold, use, and disclose personal and health information gathered when you visit our Scoresby studio or use our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, physical address, and basic emergency contact information. When you book a trial or membership, you may voluntarily disclose health or mobility details (such as joint concerns or recent surgeries) so our instructors can safely calibrate exercise machines.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide you with safe fitness and wellness services, manage your appointments, respond to enquiries, and send occasional studio announcements (which you can opt out of at any time). We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

4. WhatsApp Communication

When you enquire or book via WhatsApp, your messages are processed according to WhatsApp’s own privacy standards and encryption protocols. We use this platform solely for customer service and scheduling convenience.

5. Contacting Us About Privacy

If you have any questions or wish to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, please contact our Privacy Officer at info@aliento.com.au or call (03) 9764 1110.
