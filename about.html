We created Aliento with a simple belief: staying active and looking after yourself should feel good, sustainable, and empowering at every age.

Our Story

16+ Years Supporting Melbourne Women

Founded in Scoresby over sixteen years ago, Aliento grew out of conversations with local women who felt alienated by conventional commercial gyms. Many were tired of noisy weight rooms, crowded studios, and programs that demanded all-or-nothing exertion.

We envisioned a warm, light-filled sanctuary where women could come as they are, without worrying about what they were wearing or how athletic they felt. By introducing power-assisted Shapemaster technology alongside restorative therapies like Vibrosaun and VacuShape, we made regular movement achievable, gentle on joints, and genuinely enjoyable.

Our Philosophy

A More Comfortable Approach to Fitness

Movement does not need to be punishing to be effective. We champion gentle consistency over burnout.

Gentle on Joints

Our power-assisted machines guide each motion, taking pressure off the spine, knees, and hips while helping you work muscles smoothly and safely.

Circulation & Vitality

Gentle rhythmic movement stimulates blood flow and lymphatic drainage, helping you feel refreshed and energised rather than drained.

No Intimidation

You never have to guess how to set up equipment or feel out of place. Our team is always present to demonstrate and ensure you feel confident.

Fits Into Real Schedules

Each circuit takes roughly 35 minutes, making it simple to fit into your morning, lunch break, or afternoon routine without taking over your day.
