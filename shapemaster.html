How Shapemaster Works

Movement with Gentle Assistance

Shapemaster is an intelligent power-assisted exercise circuit originally developed to enable safe, controlled, and continuous movement. Unlike traditional gym equipment where you must push or pull heavy weight stacks alone, each Shapemaster machine moves smoothly under electric power.

You work with the machine: it assists your motion while you gently provide resistance or flex your muscles through the full range. Because there are no sudden jolts or strain on joints, it is exceptionally comfortable for women returning to exercise, those managing arthritis or past injuries, and anyone seeking sustainable tone.

Your First Visit

What to Expect During Your Session

We take time to explain everything so you feel completely at ease.

1
Personal Introduction & Goal Chat

We sit down with a cup of tea or water to understand your health history, any tender joints or back concerns, and what you would like to achieve.

2
Guided Walkthrough of Each Station

One of our experienced female team members stays beside you at each machine, showing you how to sit comfortably and adjust the speed for your body.

3
Comfortable, Modest Activewear

No need for fancy gym gear. Wear simple comfortable pants, a casual top, and clean socks. Everything is clean, private, and respectful.

4
No Soreness or Exhaustion

You leave feeling lighter, looser, and refreshed—never sore, exhausted, or unable to carry on with your day.
