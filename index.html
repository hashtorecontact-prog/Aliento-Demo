16+ Years Supporting Women’s Wellness

Since 2010, Aliento has provided Melbourne women with a calm, gentle alternative to traditional high-intensity gym environments. We understand that your body goes through different stages of life, and staying active should feel restorative rather than punishing.

Our boutique studio in Scoresby combines specialised power-assisted equipment, soothing wellness therapies, and attentive guidance. Whether you are returning to exercise after time away, managing joint sensitivities, or simply carving out dedicated me-time, you will find a supportive space where you can move comfortably at your own pace.
