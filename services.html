Each service at Aliento is selected to make regular wellness accessible, joint-friendly, and enjoyable for women of all fitness levels.

Signature Low-Impact Movement

Shapemaster Pilates

An 8-station circuit of power-assisted machines that work in synchrony with your body. By gently guiding each movement, Shapemaster allows you to tone, mobilize, and stretch without joint stress, heavy weights, or exhaustion.

Full-body 35-minute circuit targeting all major muscle groups
Ideal for joint sensitivities, back stiffness, and gentle rehabilitation
Encourages postural awareness, core engagement, and flexibility

Circulation & Targeted Walking

VacuShape

VacuShape combines gentle walking inside a vacuum-sealed chamber. The low atmospheric pressure creates a soothing drawing effect that encourages blood circulation to the lower abdomen, hips, thighs, and buttocks.

Supports natural blood and lymphatic circulation
Comfortable low walking speeds that don’t strain the joints
May help create the appearance of smoother-looking skin over time

Whole Body Vibration

Whole Body Vibration uses oscillating multi-directional frequencies to trigger reflex muscle contractions throughout your legs, core, and posture stabilizers without heavy external resistance.

Rapid 10–15 minute sessions that easily add variety to your routine
Stimulates balance, proprioception, and leg muscle engagement

Dry Heat & Relaxing Vibration

Vibrosaun

A therapeutic encapsulation combining gentle dry heat with harmonic whole-body vibration. Because your head remains outside the capsule in a stream of cool, fresh air, you enjoy all the benefits of warmth without breathing stifling hot steam.

Soothes everyday muscular tension and promotes deep relaxation
Supports circulation and natural perspiration
Head stays cool and comfortable throughout the session
