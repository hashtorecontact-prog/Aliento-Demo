The Vibrosaun Experience

Gentle Heat Without the Claustrophobia

If you have ever found traditional steam rooms or hot saunas stifling, Vibrosaun was created for you. Designed by Australian medical and engineering specialists, the Vibrosaun unit encloses only the body in a soothing capsule of dry therapeutic heat, while your head remains comfortably outside in the ambient air.

A quiet fan directs fresh, cool air over your face while your body relaxes upon a padded bed vibrating at harmonic, calming frequencies. As the gentle warmth penetrates deep into tired muscles, the vibrations soothe everyday aches, stimulate peripheral circulation, and invite a state of serene physical unwinding.

A Restorative Wellness Ritual

The Natural Benefits of Vibrosaun

Carefully calibrated temperatures and vibrations to leave you feeling deeply restored.

Eases Muscle Tightness

Dry, penetrating warmth dilates blood vessels and softens tense muscles in the lower back, hips, shoulders, and legs.

Supports Healthy Circulation

The combination of gentle heat and vibration encourages blood flow throughout the extremities, supporting natural relaxation.

Deep Physical Decompression

Lying down in a quiet, ambient room for 30 to 45 minutes provides an uninterrupted opportunity to quiet mental chatter and reset your nervous system.

Clean & Fresh Air Flow

Your head stays completely outside the heat zone, ensuring you can breathe deeply and stay cool throughout your entire treatment.

Pair Vibrosaun with Shapemaster
