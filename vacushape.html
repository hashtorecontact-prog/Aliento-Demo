Targeted Vacuum Walking

VacuShape Movement

Combining gentle walking with vacuum technology to support healthy circulation and body shaping.

The Principle of VacuShape

Movement Supported by Sub-Atmospheric Pressure

VacuShape is an enclosed walking capsule that integrates a low-impact walking treadmill within a gentle vacuum environment. While your upper body remains in the room at normal atmospheric pressure, your lower body—from the waist down—exercises inside a controlled low-pressure chamber.

The gentle vacuum effect acts like a soothing drawing motion on your venous and lymphatic systems. By encouraging blood flow directly to the adipose tissue of the thighs, buttocks, and lower abdomen while you walk at a steady, manageable pace, VacuShape helps activate areas where circulation is naturally more sluggish.

Carefully Formulated Benefits

How VacuShape Supports Your Body

Designed to be a comfortable, regular component of your weekly wellbeing plan.

Supports Circulation

The rhythmic combination of low atmospheric pressure and walking supports healthy blood flow and lymphatic drainage through the legs and pelvis.

Supports Body Shaping

Supports your body shaping efforts by concentrating activity in the hips, thighs, and lower abdomen, complementing your nutritional and lifestyle choices.

Smoother-Looking Skin

Over a consistent series of sessions, enhanced micro-circulation may help create the appearance of firmer, smoother-looking skin across target areas.

Gentle, Low-Speed Walking

You do not need to run or jog. Sessions take place at a relaxed walking pace (usually between 3 and 4.5 km/h), making it easy on the joints.

Practical Information

Who Is VacuShape Designed For?

A favourite for women seeking targeted movement without aggressive high-impact strain.

  • Women seeking to complement their healthy eating and weekly fitness routines with targeted circulation support.
  • Those who prefer a low-speed walking environment rather than stressful high-impact running or heavy lifting.
  • Women wanting to address heavy-legged feeling or sluggish circulation after sitting or standing throughout the workday.
Important Studio Note: VacuShape is designed as a wellness and fitness complement. Individual experiences vary, and we do not guarantee specific centimetre loss, medical treatment, or guaranteed weight outcomes. Certain health conditions (such as active thrombosis, severe epilepsy, or pregnancy) may have contraindications; our team will review a simple health questionnaire prior to your first session.
