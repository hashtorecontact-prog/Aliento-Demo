Studio Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Studio Environment & Etiquette

Aliento is an exclusive women-only fitness and wellness facility. We strive to maintain a serene, clean, and respectful environment for all visitors. Clean socks are required on all exercise machines. Please silence mobile phones while in the studio so all members can relax.

2. Health Screenings & Medical Disclaimers

The equipment, therapies, and information provided by Aliento are intended for general wellbeing, fitness, and relaxation. They are not medical treatments, nor are they intended to diagnose, cure, or treat specific pathological conditions. If you are pregnant or have existing cardiovascular, spinal, or surgical conditions, we encourage you to consult your medical practitioner prior to starting.

3. Appointments & Cancellations

As our equipment and therapy rooms (such as Vibrosaun and VacuShape) operate by private schedule, we kindly request at least 24 hours notice for appointment rescheduling or cancellations whenever possible.

4. Product Enquiries & Collection

Product availability shown on our website is subject to current studio inventory. Submitting an enquiry via WhatsApp places a temporary reservation with reception until confirmed and collected in person at Level 1, 7A Darryl Street, Scoresby.

5. Governing Law

These terms are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Victoria, Australia.

Women’s Fitness & Wellness Centre located in Scoresby, Victoria. Supporting women with comfortable, low-impact exercise and restorative wellness therapies for over 16 years.
